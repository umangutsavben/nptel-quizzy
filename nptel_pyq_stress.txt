1) Which of the following statements aligns with the contemporary view of stress?

   Options:
   - Stress is primarily a physiological response
   - Stress is a subjective phenomenon dependent on individual appraisals
   - Stress is caused solely by external environmental factors
   - Stress is a non-specific response of the body

   CORRECT: Stress is a subjective phenomenon dependent on individual appraisals


2) Which of the following is NOT a possible way an event can be appraised during primary appraisal?

   Options:
   - Irrelevant
   - Relevant but not threatening
   - Stressful
   - Coping

   CORRECT: Coping


3) What type of appraisal is future-oriented and involves the possibility of harm or loss?

   Options:
   - Harm-loss appraisal
   - Threat appraisal
   - Challenge appraisal
   - Secondary appraisal

   CORRECT: Threat appraisal


4) What is the relationship between reappraisal and earlier appraisals?

   Options:
   - Reappraisal replaces earlier appraisals entirely
   - Reappraisal modifies earlier appraisals based on new information
   - Reappraisal is unrelated to earlier appraisals
   - Reappraisal only occurs before primary appraisal

   CORRECT: Reappraisal modifies earlier appraisals based on new information


5) What is the role of cultural norms in stress perception?

   Options:
   - Cultural norms have no influence on stress
   - Cultural norms only affect physical health
   - Cultural norms can shape how individuals appraise and experience stress
   - Cultural norms eliminate stress entirely

   CORRECT: Cultural norms can shape how individuals appraise and experience stress


6) Choosing between surgery and radiation treatment for cancer is an example of:

   Options:
   - Approach-approach
   - Approach-avoidance
   - Avoidance-avoidance
   - Conflict
   - None of these

   CORRECT: Avoidance-avoidance


7) What is the term used to describe diseases that may result from prolonged stress, according to General Adaptation?

   Options:
   - Diseases of adaptation
   - Diseases of resistance
   - Diseases of exhaustion
   - Diseases of alarm

   CORRECT: Diseases of adaptation


8) What is the primary function of the SAM system during stress?

   Options:
   - To release cortisol for long-term stress
   - To increase heart rate, blood pressure, and energy for immediate action
   - To promote relaxation and digestion
   - To reduce the body's resistance to stress

   CORRECT: To increase heart rate, blood pressure, and energy for immediate action


9) Which system is responsible for coping with chronic stress?

   Options:
   - SAM system
   - Somatic nervous system
   - Sympathetic nervous system
   - HPA system

   CORRECT: HPA system


10) What is the role of oxytocin in the tend-and-befriend response?

    Options:
    - It increases heart rate and blood pressure
    - It promotes relaxation, reduces anxiety, and encourages bonding behavior
    - It enhances memory and learning
    - It increases the release of cortisol

    CORRECT: It promotes relaxation, reduces anxiety, and encourages bonding behavior

1) How does chronic stress contribute to the development of atherosclerosis?

   Options:

   - By promoting the build-up of fatty plaques in arterial walls through elevated cortisol and adrenaline levels
   - By reducing the heart's ability to pump blood efficiently
   - By causing inflammation of the heart muscle

   CORRECT: By promoting the build-up of fatty plaques in arterial walls through elevated cortisol and adrenaline levels


2) How does Broken Heart Syndrome differ from a heart attack?

   Options:
   - Broken Heart Syndrome is caused by blocked arteries, while a heart attack is caused by stress hormones
   - Broken Heart Syndrome shows no evidence of blocked coronary arteries, unlike a heart attack
   - Broken Heart Syndrome is a chronic condition, while a heart attack is an acute event
   - Broken Heart Syndrome only occurs in men, while heart attacks are more common in women

   CORRECT: Broken Heart Syndrome shows no evidence of blocked coronary arteries, unlike a heart attack


3) Which of the following statements about Broken Heart Syndrome is TRUE?

   Options:
   - It only occurs in individuals with pre-existing heart conditions
   - It is caused by long-term exposure to low levels of stress
   - It is more common in men than in women
   - It can occur in healthy individuals with no history of heart disease

   CORRECT: It can occur in healthy individuals with no history of heart disease


4) How can excess cholesterol caused by stress contribute to cardiovascular diseases?

   Options:
   - By accumulating as fats in the arteries, leading to blockages and heart attacks
   - By improving blood flow and oxygen supply to the heart
   - By increasing the production of red blood cells, thickening the blood
   - By reducing the heart's ability to pump blood efficiently

   CORRECT: By accumulating as fats in the arteries, leading to blockages and heart attacks

   Options:
   - Excessive competitiveness
   - Time urgency

   CORRECT: Anger and hostility

   Options:
   - Both acute and chronic stress enhance immune function
   - Acute stress enhances immune function, while chronic stress suppresses it
   - Acute stress suppresses immune function, while chronic stress enhances it
   - Both acute and chronic stress suppress immune function

   CORRECT: Acute stress enhances immune function, while chronic stress suppresses it

   Options:
   - Acute time-limited stressors like public speaking

   - PTG emphasizes stability after trauma, while resilience emphasizes transformation

   CORRECT: PTG involves profound personal improvement, while resilience involves returning to baseline functioning

   CORRECT: Automatic ruminative thoughts are intrusive and distressing, while reflective thoughts are conscious and deliberate

   Options:
   - It prevents the occurrence of trauma

1) According to the VIA classification, the virtue of 'humanity' includes which of the following character strengths?

   Options:
   - Fairness
   - Kindness
   - Creativity
   - Modesty

   CORRECT: Kindness


2) Peterson and Seligman (2004) identified ........ character strengths and grouped them into ........ virtues:

   Options:
   - 24, 6
   - 25, 6
   - 24, 5
   - 23, 6

   CORRECT: 24, 6


3) The Gallup's Strengths Finder came up with ........ most prevalent talents/strengths displayed specifically in the work setting.

   Options:
   - 35
   - 34
   - 30
   - 24

   CORRECT: 34


4) According to Gallup's Strengths Finder, which of the following creates a strength?

   Options:
   - Talent
   - Skills
   - Knowledge
   - Skills and knowledge combined with talent

   CORRECT: Skills and knowledge combined with talent


5) Which of the following is associated with the following statement 'Top achievers fully recognize their talents and build on them to develop strengths'?

   Options:
   - Value in action classification of character strengths
   - Gallup's Strengths Finder
   - Seligman's PERMA model
   - Flow

   CORRECT: Gallup's Strengths Finder


6) Which of the following is NOT a dimension of Gallup's Strengths Finder classification?

   Options:
   - Executing
   - Influencing
   - Relationship building
   - Creative thinking

   CORRECT: Creative thinking


7) Which of the following is NOT a way to identify talents?

   Options:
   - Spontaneous reactions
   - Yearnings
   - Slow learning
   - Satisfaction and enjoyment of activities

   CORRECT: Slow learning


8) Autotelic Personality is associated with:

   Options:
   - Internal locus of control
   - Resilience
   - Flow
   - Psychological hardiness

   CORRECT: Flow


9) What is the nature of activities that lead to flow experience?

   Options:
   - They are extrinsically rewarding
   - They are not challenging enough
   - They are intrinsically rewarding
   - They are boring

   CORRECT: They are intrinsically rewarding


10) What type of activities are less likely to be associated with flow experience?

    Options:
    - Sports and dancing
    - Creative arts and hobbies
    - Housework and watching TV
    - Socializing and studying

    CORRECT: Housework and watching TV

WEEK 11
================================================================================

1) "What a man can be, he must be" is associated with which of the following:

   Options:
   - Esteem need
   - Love and belongingness need
   - Physiological need
   - Self-actualization need

   CORRECT: Self-actualization need


2) What is the main difference between self-actualization needs and other needs in Maslow's hierarchy?

   Options:
   - Self-actualization needs are growth needs while other needs are deficit needs
   - Self-actualization needs are deficit needs while other needs are growth needs
   - Self-actualization needs are related to basic survival while other needs are related to personal growth
   - Self-actualization needs are related to social belongingness while other needs are related to power and control

   CORRECT: Self-actualization needs are growth needs while other needs are deficit needs


3) According to Carl Rogers, which of the following is an obstacle in achieving our 'real-self'?

   Options:
   - Lack of desire
   - Stuck in fulfilling lower needs
   - Societal conditions of worth
   - None of these

   CORRECT: Societal conditions of worth


4) According to Carl Rogers, inconsistency between our real self and ideal self creates:

   Options:
   - Psychosis
   - Neurosis
   - Organismic valuing
   - None of these

   CORRECT: Neurosis


5) Which of the following theory explains human motivation based on the basic psychological need fulfilment?

   Options:
   - Organismic valuing theory
   - Functional descriptive model
   - PERMA model
   - Self-determination theory

   CORRECT: Self-determination theory


6) A student completes an assignment independently, but only because of fear of shaming self or because of guilt about the consequences of not completing the assignment. This is an example of............. type of extrinsic motivation:

   Options:
   - External regulation
   - Introjection
   - Identification
   - Integration

   CORRECT: Introjection


7) .......... theory assumes that an extrinsic reward decreases intrinsic motivation when a person attributes his/her performance to the extrinsic reward.

   Options:
   - Cognitive evaluation theory
   - Over-justification theory
   - Self-determination theory
   - Theory of actualizing tendency

   CORRECT: Cognitive evaluation theory


8) .......... and .......... are more autonomous and internalized forms of extrinsic motivation:

   Options:
   - Identification and integration
   - External and introjection
   - Identification and introjection
   - External and integration

   CORRECT: Identification and integration


9) How can the need for relatedness be fulfilled in a work environment?

   Options:
   - By providing challenging tasks
   - By encouraging competition among employees
   - By devoting time for team-building activities
   - By providing financial incentives

   CORRECT: By devoting time for team-building activities


10) The term "self-actualization" was first coined by:

    Options:
    - Maslow
    - Rogers
    - Kurt Goldstein
    - Martin Seligman

    CORRECT: Kurt Goldstein

1) Life has meaning under all circumstances, even the most miserable ones. This statement is associated with:

   Options:
   - Cognitive therapy
   - Mindfulness based therapy
   - Behavioral therapy
   - Logotherapy

   CORRECT: Logotherapy


2) Mattering is connected to which of the following?

   Options:
   - Coherence
   - Purpose
   - Significance
   - All of these

   CORRECT: All of these


3) Which of the following is a motivational component of meaning in life?

   Options:
   - Coherence
   - Purpose
   - Significance
   - Mattering

   CORRECT: Purpose


4) Which of the following is associated with George T. Doran?

   Options:
   - Intrinsic goals
   - Extrinsic goals
   - SMART goals
   - Self-actualization

   CORRECT: SMART goals


5) Coherence dimension of meaning in life is value neutral and:

   Options:
   - Descriptive
   - Normative
   - Evaluative
   - None of these

   CORRECT: Descriptive


6) According to research, which of the following dimensions of meaning in life may diminish death anxiety?

   Options:
   - Coherence
   - Purpose
   - Significance
   - None of these

   CORRECT: Significance


7) What did Frankl believe is the last of human freedoms, even in the most challenging circumstances?

   Options:
   - Freedom to pursue material success
   - Freedom to avoid suffering
   - Freedom to achieve external goals
   - Freedom to choose one's attitude in any given circumstance

   CORRECT: Freedom to choose one's attitude in any given circumstance


8) What is one way Frankl suggested individuals can discover meaning in life?

   Options:
   - Creating a work or doing a deed
   - Avoiding all forms of suffering
   - Pursuing external achievements only
   - Experiencing natural beauty without any action

   CORRECT: Creating a work or doing a deed


9) Why might satisfaction from extrinsic goals not last?

   Options:
   - Extrinsic goals are inherently unsatisfying
   - People quickly adapt to extrinsic goals
   - Satisfaction from extrinsic goals is dependent on external factors
   - Extrinsic goals are not valued by one's culture

   CORRECT: People quickly adapt to extrinsic goals


10) ..................... are strongly shaped by cultural and societal beliefs and norms.

    Options:
    - Intrinsic goals
    - Extrinsic goals
    - Both intrinsic and extrinsic goals
    - None of these

    CORRECT: Extrinsic goals
